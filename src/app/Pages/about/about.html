The Broken Glass Band

The Broken Glass Band is an electrifying electroswing ensemble that combines retrofuturistic melodies with dynamic rhythms, creating a genre-defying sound that transcends time and place. Drawing inspiration from classic swing and jazz, the band infuses modern electronic elements, crafting tracks that are both nostalgic and forward-thinking. With their energetic live performances and innovative arrangements, The Broken Glass Band has become a must-see act

From their debut at the illustrious Parisian nightclub Le Moulin Rouge to headlining major festivals worldwide, The Broken Glass Band has earned a reputation for pushing boundaries and captivating audiences with their unique sound. Their music is not just listened to; it's danced to, swayed to, and felt in every beat. As pioneers of electroswing, they continue to evolve the genre while staying true to their roots, ensuring that each performance leaves a lasting impression.
